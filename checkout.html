Before you order

Delivery, payment and returns

Delivery

We deliver nationwide. Orders are processed within 1–2 business days and typically arrive in 2–5 business days, depending on your location. Delivery fees are based on your address and confirmed before payment. We’ll update you when your order is dispatched.

Payment

Pay by debit or credit card, bank transfer, or secure online payment. Payment must be confirmed before an order is processed.

Returns and exchanges

Request a return or exchange within 7 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unused, undamaged, and in original packaging. Jewelry must be in its original condition; sale and final-sale items are excluded. Contact us within 48 hours about a wrong, damaged, or defective item. Delivery fees are non-refundable unless the issue was our error.
